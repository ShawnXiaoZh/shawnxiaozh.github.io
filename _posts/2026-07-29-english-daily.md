## 单词与表达

1. **I knew I would regret it if I didn't speak to her** — 我知道不跟她说话我会后悔
    - I knew I would regret it if I didn't speak to her.
    - 原笔记写的 if I wouldn't have spoken 是常见错句：if 从句里不用 would。虚拟语气正确写法是 if I hadn't spoken to her, I would have regretted it。

2. **all of them** — 全部、所有这些
    - Which ones do you want? — All of them.
    - 注意 all of them 后面必须是代词或带限定词的名词（all of the files），直接说 all files 时不加 of。

3. **it was ready** — 已经准备好了
    - By the time we arrived, dinner was ready.
    - ready 描述状态而非动作。人也可以 ready：Are you ready to go? 强调"随时可以"用 ready to go。

4. **other than that** — 除此之外
    - The room was small. Other than that, the hotel was great.
    - 用来把一个例外圈出来，剩下的一概肯定。近义 apart from that（英式）、besides that。放句首要加逗号。

5. **these things** — 这些事情
    - These things take time.
    - 原笔记记成 these think，是听音误记。These things take time 是很好用的安慰句，意思是"这种事急不来"。

6. **be sick of sth** — 厌倦、受够了
    - I'm sick of eating the same thing every day.
    - 后接名词或动名词。程度递增：tired of（有点烦）< sick of（受够了）< sick and tired of（忍无可忍）。

7. **it's never-ending** — 没完没了
    - The laundry is never-ending.
    - 形容词写作 never-ending。抱怨重复性琐事时最常用。近义 it never stops / there's no end to it。

8. **intertwined** — 交织在一起、密不可分
    - Their lives have been intertwined since childhood.
    - 字面是"缠绕在一起"，抽象用法指关系或事情纠缠难分。比 connected 强得多。

9. **vicious cycle** — 恶性循环
    - I sleep badly, so I drink more coffee, so I sleep badly — a vicious cycle.
    - 反面是 virtuous cycle（良性循环）。注意别拼成 viscous（黏稠的），这两个词长得很像。

10. **try it out** — 试一试
    - The new place opened downstairs — let's try it out.
    - out 强调"实际用一次看看效果"。试穿衣服是 try it on，两者别混。

11. **distribute vs disturb** — 分发 vs 打扰
    - Could you distribute the handouts? / Sorry to disturb you.
    - 两个词只是长得像，意思毫无关系。酒店门牌上的 Do not disturb 就是这个 disturb。

12. **more of** — 更偏向于、更像是
    - It's more of a hobby than a job.
    - more of a A than a B 是很好用的句型，用来修正对方的定性，而不是直接否定。

13. **dissimulate** — 掩饰真实情感或意图
    - When people dissimulate, they hide their true feelings, intentions, or nature.
    - 书面语，日常几乎不用，读小说会遇到。口语说 hide it / put on a front。近义 dissemble；反义 be upfront。

14. **by the time** — 等到……的时候
    - By the time we got there, the kitchen had closed.
    - 后面接从句且不用将来时（By the time you arrive, I'll have finished）。主句常配完成时，因为强调"在那之前已经发生"。

15. **dwindle** — 逐渐减少
    - Might I also add, the number of drinking friends has been dwindling.
    - 强调"慢慢变少"的过程，常用进行时。近义 shrink（体积缩小）、taper off（逐渐停止）。

16. **ignorance is bliss** — 不知道反而幸福
    - I didn't read the reviews before going. Ignorance is bliss.
    - 固定谚语，多用于自嘲。完整原句是 where ignorance is bliss, 'tis folly to be wise。

17. **it doesn't sit right with me** — 我总觉得这样不太对
    - Something about that deal doesn't sit right with me.
    - 表达直觉上的不适，说不出具体理由。比 I disagree 柔和，适合提出模糊但真实的顾虑。

18. **I'm still around** — 我还在（没走）
    - I'm still around if you need anything.
    - around 指"在附近、还在线"。下班道别常说 I'm around tomorrow if you need me。

19. **come across sth** — 偶然发现、无意中看到
    - I went into the library this afternoon to have a look at a newspaper and I came across something really interesting.
    - 强调"不是刻意找的"。come across as 是另一个意思：给人留下某种印象（He comes across as shy）。

20. **shall we … then?** — 那我们就……吧？
    - So everything is ready — shall we go then?
    - 句末的 then 承接前面的结论，起推进作用，相当于"那就……"。英式口语高频。

21. **spoil ourselves** — 犒劳一下自己、享受一下
    - We've been working hard — let's spoil ourselves this weekend.
    - 也说 treat ourselves（更常用）。注意 spoil a child 是"宠坏孩子"，spoil the ending 是"剧透"。

22. **refreshments** — 茶点、简单饮食
    - Free refreshments will be provided.
    - 指会议或活动上提供的饮料点心，不是正餐。只有复数形式，正式场合的固定用词。

23. **that sounds like a bargain** — 听起来很划算
    - Twenty dollars for all that? That sounds like a bargain to me.
    - bargain 作名词是"划算的买卖"。近义 a good deal、a steal（便宜得离谱）。

24. **keen** — 有兴趣的、热衷的
    - So what else? I'm feeling quite keen now.
    - 英式常用，美式更爱说 excited / eager。keen on sth 是"喜欢某事"：I'm not keen on spicy food.

25. **if only…** — 要是……就好了
    - If only I had more time.
    - 表达不能实现的愿望，后面用过去式（现在）或过去完成时（过去）。也可以单独说一句 If only. 表示"要是能这样就好了"。

26. **a consuming passion** — 强烈到占据全部心思的热爱
    - My consuming passion for cycling keeps me training every day.
    - consuming 字面是"吞噬性的"，形容爱好几乎占满生活。近义 all-consuming。

27. **go for sth** — 选择、决定要
    - So I think we'll go for the Main Hall. How much would that cost?
    - 点餐、选方案都能用：I'll go for the fish. 这里用 would 而不是 does，因为还没最终决定，是假设性询问。

28. **it's got** — 它有（英式口语）
    - It's got very good facilities — good quality cookers and fridges and so on.
    - have got 在英式口语里比 have 更常用，强调现在拥有的状态。美式更常直接说 it has。

29. **sort sth out** — 处理好、搞定
    - That's Mr Evans — he'll sort that out with you.
    - 英式高频动词短语，涵盖"安排、解决、整理"。美式更常说 take care of it / figure it out。

30. **out of** — 从……里面（拿走）
    - I had some things stolen out of my bag yesterday.
    - 强调"从容器内部取出"，比单用 from 更具体。报案的标准说法：I want to report a theft.

31. **come over** — 过来（到我这里）
    - Do you want to come over tonight? / Come over when you're free.
    - 特指来说话人所在的地方，通常是家里。去别人那儿用 go over；随便逛过去用 drop by / pop over。

32. **get out to somewhere** — （特意）跑到较远的地方去
    - There are some really lovely things around here, but you need to get out to the small towns.
    - out 强调"离开市区、路途不近"。近义 venture out。语气上暗示"值得专门跑一趟"。

33. **drop sth off** — 顺路送到、放下
    - We went to see friends, we dropped off the food, and now we're going to head home.
    - 指把东西（或人）送到某地就走。接人是 pick sb up，两者是一对。

34. **compliment sb on sth** — 称赞某人某方面
    - He complimented her on her dress.
    - 注意拼写：compliment 是称赞，complement 是补足、搭配得好。发音相同，意思完全不同。

35. **hide and seek** — 捉迷藏
    - We're playing hide and seek.
    - 固定搭配不加冠词。玩这个游戏用 play hide and seek。

36. **it's not as useful as it could be** — 它本可以更有用
    - The app works, but it's not as useful as it could be.
    - not as … as it could be 是很委婉的批评方式：不说"不好"，只说"没发挥出应有的水平"。

37. **outspoken** — 直言不讳的
    - She's quite outspoken in meetings.
    - 中性偏褒，指敢公开表达看法。贬义的"说话不过脑"是 blunt 或 tactless。

38. **what is there to do?** — 有什么可以玩的？
    - We're in town for the weekend — what is there to do?
    - 问当地活动的固定句型。也可以说 What's there to do around here? 回答常用 There's plenty to do.

39. **fulfillment** — 成就感；履行
    - She finds fulfillment in her work. / the fulfilment of a promise
    - 两个意思：内心的满足感，以及承诺、订单的履行。英式拼 fulfilment，美式拼 fulfillment。

40. **maiden name** — （女性）婚前姓
    - What's your maiden name?
    - 常见于银行和证件的安全问题。婚后改的姓叫 married name；不改姓叫 keep her maiden name。

41. **I was going to ask you…** — 我本来想问你……
    - I was going to ask you if you're free tomorrow.
    - was going to 表示"原本打算"，用来软化提问，听起来不那么突兀。近义 I meant to ask you…

42. **be scattered all over…** — 分散在各地
    - They're all my college friends, but they're scattered all over the country.
    - scatter 是"撒开、分散"，被动形式描述人分布在很多地方。近义 spread out across。

43. **more or less** — 差不多、基本上
    - The report is more or less finished.
    - 用来给陈述留余地，避免把话说死。近义 pretty much（更口语）、roughly（偏数量）。

44. **come up with sth** — 想出（办法、点子）
    - She came up with a great idea.
    - 强调"从无到有想出来"。注意过去式是 came up with，原笔记漏了时态。近义 think of（更随意）。

45. **props** — 赞扬、认可
    - Props to the team for turning that around so fast.
    - 来自 proper respect，年轻化的口语。近义 kudos、hats off to。正式场合仍用 credit goes to…

46. **intend to do sth** — 打算做某事
    - I was intending to call you yesterday.
    - 过去进行时 was intending 暗示"本来打算但没做成"，常用来道歉。比 I wanted to 更正式一点。

47. **spray** — 喷（液体）
    - She sprayed the perfume. / mosquito spray
    - 动词和名词同形。日常搭配：sunscreen spray（防晒喷雾）、bug spray（驱蚊液）。原笔记 mosquite 应作 mosquito。

48. **I can't remember exactly** — 我记不太清了
    - I can't remember exactly, but it was around 2019.
    - 承认记不清但仍给个大概，比单说 I don't know 有用。搭配 around / roughly 一起用最自然。

49. **welp** — 行吧、那好吧
    - Welp, I guess we'll try again tomorrow.
    - well 的口语变体，表示接受一个不太理想的结果，带一点认命的幽默。只用于非正式场合。

50. **other than / apart from** — 除了（= except）
    - I didn't listen to much other than when she was addressing me directly.
    - address 的本义是"把东西朝某个对象送过去"：对人是"向某人讲话"，对事是"处理某件事"。这里 addressing me 指"直接对我说话"。

51. **side hustle** — 副业
    - I'm thinking of getting a side hustle.
    - 指本职之外的赚钱项目，带积极进取的意味。中性说法是 a second job；hustle 本身有"折腾、拼"的味道。

52. **herd** — 兽群（牛、象等）
    - We saw an elephant herd at the waterhole.
    - 不同动物有不同集合名词：a herd of elephants、a flock of birds、a school of fish、a pack of wolves。

53. **宾语从句语序** — 从句里要用陈述语序（主语 + 动词），不能再用疑问语序
    - I know why he leaves. ✅ / I know why does he leave. ❌
    - Can you explain how it works? ✅ / Can you explain how does it work? ❌
    - 这是中文母语者最高频的错误之一。疑问词后面立刻跟主语，助动词 do/does 一律去掉，时态回归正常动词。

## 常见句型

1. **be … apart** — 相隔（时间或距离）
    - They are about six weeks apart.
    - 说年龄差、时间差、距离都能用：The two towns are 30 km apart. 兄弟姐妹年龄差常这么说。

2. **a good match with** — 和……很搭
    - This cucumber salad is a good match with spicy food.
    - 食物搭配也常说 pairs well with（更地道）：This wine pairs well with seafood.

3. **I would love to…** — 我很想……
    - I would love to have six kids.
    - 比 I want to 更热情也更礼貌。婉拒时也很好用：I'd love to, but I can't.

4. **growing up** — 从小到大、在成长过程中
    - Growing up, I had three siblings.
    - 放句首作状语，等于 when I was a child。siblings 是兄弟姐妹的统称，不分性别。

5. **picture sth** — 想象、设想
    - What do you picture family life to look like?
    - picture 作动词是"在脑中描绘"，比 imagine 更具画面感。常说 I can't picture it（我想象不出来）。

6. **be on a call** — 在通话中
    - Anytime we're all on a call, someone's connection drops.
    - 电话或视频会议中用 on a call。注意介词是 on，不是 in。开会中是 in a meeting。

7. **downstairs** — 在楼下
    - I usually eat downstairs.
    - 本身已含介词，不能说 in downstairs。对应 upstairs。楼下邻居是 the people downstairs。

8. **gotta / down there** — 得去…… / 在下面那边
    - We gotta go help fix a toddler issue down there now.
    - gotta 是 have got to 的口语缩写，只用于说话和聊天，不写进正式文字。down there 指"下面某个地方"。

9. **catch up on sleep** — 补觉
    - I'm catching up on sleep this weekend.
    - catch up on 用于补落下的东西：sleep、work、emails、a TV show。补觉也说 sleep in（睡懒觉）。

10. **stay up late** — 熬夜
    - I stayed up too late too many times this week.
    - stay up 是"不去睡"，通宵是 stay up all night / pull an all-nighter。被吵醒睡不着是 be up。

11. **goodwill** — 善意、好意
    - They did it out of goodwill.
    - 常搭配 out of goodwill（出于好意）、a goodwill gesture（示好的举动）。商业语境里还指"商誉"。

12. **it seems to be** — 似乎是这样
    - Is it fixed? — It seems to be.
    - 回答时可以省略后面的内容，只说 It seems to be. 相当于中文的"看起来是的"。

13. **come out** — 出现、显露；发布
    - The sun came out in the afternoon. / The new season comes out next month.
    - 一词多义，靠主语判断：天气、污渍、真相、新产品，都能 come out。

14. **second time around** — 第二次（再来一次时）
    - It was much easier the second time around.
    - around 强调"再次经历同一件事"。也说 this time around（这一回）。

15. **how soon…?** — 还要多久……？
    - How soon will the baby come after the water breaks?
    - how soon 问"多快会发生"，how long 问"持续多久"。原笔记的 water brakes 应作 water breaks（羊水破了）。

16. **how far along** — 进展到哪一步了（尤指孕期）
    - How far along are you? — About seven months.
    - 问孕期进度的固定说法。泛用于项目进度：How far along is the migration?

17. **in between** — 在（两者）中间的空档
    - So you have time in between to catch a breath.
    - 指两件事之间的间隙。catch a breath 是"喘口气"，也说 catch your breath。

18. **can't wait** — 迫不及待
    - I can't wait to see them.
    - 后接 to + 动词原形，或 for + 名词：I can't wait for the weekend. 表达期待最自然的说法。

19. **barely** — 几乎不
    - We barely slept.
    - barely 本身就是否定意义，不能再加 not。近义 hardly、scarcely。程度上 barely 强调"勉强达到或几乎没有"。

20. **a lot sooner** — 早得多
    - It happened a lot sooner than we expected.
    - a lot 用来加强比较级，比 much 口语。同类：a lot better、a lot more expensive。

21. **the other day** — 前几天
    - I ran into him the other day.
    - 指最近某天，不具体说是哪天，通常在一两周内。同类模糊时间：the other week、a while back。

22. **get to do sth** — 有机会做（带幸运或享受的语气）
    - I get to eat spicy food all the time.
    - 和 have to（不得不）正好相反：I have to work weekends（无奈）vs I get to work from home（幸运）。

23. **分数表达** — one third of…；nine out of ten
    - One third of the class was absent. / Nine out of ten people prefer it.
    - 分子用基数词，分母用序数词；分子大于一时分母加 s（two thirds）。口语里 X out of Y 更常用也更好懂。

24. **it's worth a lot** — 很值钱、很有价值
    - That advice is worth a lot.
    - worth 后面直接接名词，不加 of。同类：worth a try（值得一试）、worth it（值得）。

25. **onwards** — 继续向前
    - He waved and walked onwards.
    - 英式拼 onwards，美式拼 onward。也用于时间：from 2020 onwards（从 2020 年起）。

26. **on her own** — 自己、独自
    - Sometimes she wakes up on her own.
    - 强调"没人帮忙"。近义 by herself。alone 只是"独自一人"，不含"靠自己完成"的意思。

27. **undivided attention** — 全神贯注、心无旁骛
    - I can't give it my full undivided attention anymore.
    - 固定搭配 give sb your undivided attention。undivided 就是"没被分走的"，所以带娃时说这句特别贴切。

28. **a stretch** — 一段连续的时间
    - I think I might get a two-hour stretch here.
    - 指不被打断的一整段时间，常用于睡眠：a four-hour stretch of sleep。也指一段路或一段时期。

29. **wrap up** — 收尾、结束
    - I think I'll wrap up this video here.
    - to finish / to bring something to an end。会议、视频、项目都能 wrap up。名词 a wrap 表示拍摄结束：That's a wrap.

30. **here we are** — 我们到了
    - Here we are — this is the place.
    - 到达目的地时说。注意区分 there you go（给你/就这样）、here you are（东西递给你）。

31. **flavorful** — 味道浓郁的
    - The broth is so flavorful.
    - 美式拼 flavorful，英式拼 flavourful。近义 tasty（好吃）、rich（浓厚）。夸汤头最贴切的就是 flavorful。

32. **phenomenal** — 非凡的、了不起的
    - The view was phenomenal.
    - 强烈的褒义词，比 great 重得多。原笔记记作"太不寻常了"，实际语气是"太棒了"，而不是中性的"不寻常"。

33. **home delivery** — 送货上门
    - They do home deliveries now.
    - 外卖更常说 food delivery、takeaway（英式）、takeout（美式）。点外卖的动作是 order in。

34. **intervals / periods** — 节、时段
    - Each game is played in three 20-minute periods.
    - 冰球分三节（periods），足球两个 halves，篮球四节 quarters。interval 在英式里还指演出中场休息。

35. **instead** — 反而、作为替代
    - We didn't take a taxi, we walked instead.
    - 单独用时放句末。instead of 后接被替代的东西：We walked instead of taking a taxi.

36. **step forward** — 上前一步；挺身而出
    - I stepped forward to get a better look.
    - 字面是往前走一步，引申义是"主动站出来"：Nobody stepped forward to take responsibility.

37. **the catch** — 隐藏的条件、附带的问题
    - The offer sounds great — the only catch is you have to pay in advance.
    - 指看起来很好的事情背后的限制条件。近义 drawback、limitation。常说 What's the catch?（有什么陷阱？）

38. **by the time** — 等到……的时候
    - By the time we got there, it was around 6 or 7 in the morning.
    - 后接从句，主句常用完成时表示"在那之前已发生"。注意 by the time 后不用将来时。

39. **make it to somewhere** — 成功抵达
    - We made it to the summit.
    - make it 强调"克服困难才到达"，也用于赶上：I made it to the meeting on time. 单纯到达用 arrive / get to。

40. **bucket list** — 遗愿清单、人生必做清单
    - Seeing the northern lights is on my bucket list.
    - 源自 kick the bucket（去世）。常说 tick sth off my bucket list（划掉一项）。

41. **keep it up** — 保持下去、继续加油
    - Great progress — keep it up!
    - 鼓励对方维持现状的固定说法。近义 keep going（更强调别停）、keep at it（强调坚持啃难题）。

42. **the road ahead** — 前方道路
    - Follow the road ahead in a straight line.
    - 字面指前面的路，比喻义是"未来的路"：The road ahead won't be easy. 问路对话里常出现。

43. **that way** — 这样一来
    - Tell me your address. That way, I can send you the documents.
    - 用来说明前一句动作带来的好处，起因果连接作用。近义 so that…，但 that way 更口语、断句更轻。

44. **the downside** — 不利之处、弊端
    - The downside of modern life is that we're always reachable.
    - 对应词是 the upside（好处）。近义 drawback。常说 the downside is that…

45. **subsequent** — 随后的
    - For several subsequent days I saw little of Mr Rochester.
    - 书面语，指"接下来的、后续的"。口语说 the following days / the next few days。副词 subsequently 意为"后来"。

46. **bend to the right** — 向右转弯（路自己拐弯）
    - Take the main path ahead of you, go straight on, and keep going till the path bends to the right.
    - bend 是路本身的弯曲；turn right 是你主动转向。听力指路题里这个区别很关键。

47. **benefit from sth** — 从……中获益
    - We all benefit in many ways from regular exercise.
    - benefit from 是受益，benefit sb 是使某人受益（The change benefits everyone）。名词短语：the benefits of exercise。

48. **hardly ever** — 几乎从不
    - I hardly ever eat breakfast.
    - 等于 almost never。hardly 本身是否定词，所以不能再加 not。位置放在主要动词前。

49. **not least** — 尤其是、特别是
    - It's an expensive city, not least because of the rent.
    - 等于 especially，语气正式，常用来引出最重要的那个理由。别和 last but not least（最后但同样重要）混淆。

50. **sit still** — 坐着不动
    - They have to sit still and focus on one thing.
    - still 在这里是形容词"静止的"，不是副词"仍然"。同类：stand still、hold still（别动，拍照或打针时说）。

51. **that aspect of sth** — ……的那个方面
    - That aspect of handwriting had never occurred to me before.
    - occur to sb 是"（想法）突然进入某人脑中"，主语是想法不是人：It never occurred to me that…

52. **associate A with B** — 把 A 和 B 联系起来
    - I'd never associated spatial awareness with handwriting either.
    - 固定介词是 with。名词 association 指这种心理关联：I have good associations with that song.

53. **more to do with / in relation to** — 更多是关于…… / 相对于……而言
    - I thought spatial awareness was more to do with knowing where you are in relation to objects or other people.
    - in relation to 用于比较两者的大小、状态或位置关系。more to do with 用来纠正对方的理解侧重点，很委婉。

54. **it's not just … that …** — 重要的不只是……
    - It's not just the looks that matter.
    - 这是强调句型 it is … that …，把 not just 放进去，用来突出"还有别的因素"。后面常接 it's also…

55. **coordinate movement** — 协调动作
    - Handwriting helps children coordinate movement.
    - coordinate 作动词是"使协调配合"。名词 coordination：hand-eye coordination（手眼协调）是固定搭配。

56. **anyone in particular** — 有特定的某个人吗
    - So is there anyone in particular I should speak to there?
    - in particular 放在名词后表示"特指的"。同类：anything in particular（有什么特别的吗）。

57. **what are you up to?** — 你在忙什么？
    - What are you up to this weekend?
    - 问对方在做什么或有什么安排，比 What are you doing? 更随意。缩写 wuu2 只用于短信聊天。

58. **it's everyday, bro** — 天天都这样
    - Traffic like this? It's everyday, bro.
    - 注意区分：everyday 是形容词"日常的"（everyday life），every day 是"每天"。这句口语里当"稀松平常"用。
